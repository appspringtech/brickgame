# Game Design Document

## Brick Construction Game for 3-Year-Olds

### Overview

This game is specifically designed for 3-year-old children to provide a safe, engaging, and educational experience that develops motor skills and spatial awareness through simple brick construction.

## Design Philosophy

### Core Principles

1. **No Failure States**: There is no wrong way to play. Every action is valid.
2. **Positive Feedback**: All interactions result in encouraging visual and audio feedback.
3. **Simplicity**: Mouse-only controls with drag and drop - no keyboard required.
4. **Visual Clarity**: Large, colorful blocks that are easy to see and control.
5. **Safety First**: No text, no scores, no ads, no internet connection required.

### Target Audience

- **Age**: 3 years old
- **Motor Skills**: Developing fine motor control
- **Cognitive**: Understanding spatial relationships
- **Experience**: May be first computer game

## Game Mechanics

### Brick Selection

- **Action**: Click on any brick in the toolbar
- **Result**: A new brick appears in the building area
- **Feedback**: Gentle pickup sound effect

### Brick Placement

- **Action**: Brick follows mouse cursor
- **Result**: Brick can be moved anywhere in building area
- **Feedback**: Visual sparkles when placed

### Snap-to-Grid

- **Purpose**: Makes placement easier and more predictable
- **Grid Size**: 30px (large enough for easy alignment)
- **Behavior**: Bricks automatically align to grid when released

### Snap-to-Brick

- **Purpose**: Enables easy stacking and building
- **Threshold**: 40px (generous for developing motor skills)
- **Behavior**: Bricks snap to nearby bricks when close
- **Feedback**: Special snap sound and animation

### Brick Movement

- **Action**: Click and drag any placed brick
- **Result**: Brick can be repositioned
- **No Limits**: Bricks can overlap, stack, or be anywhere

## Visual Design

### Color Palette

Seven vibrant, high-contrast colors optimized for young children:
- **Red**: #FF6B6B
- **Blue**: #4ECDC4
- **Green**: #95E1D3
- **Yellow**: #FFE66D
- **Purple**: #B565D8
- **Orange**: #FFA07A
- **Pink**: #FFB6D9

### Brick Sizes

Five different sizes to encourage exploration:
- **Small**: 100x60px (2 studs)
- **Medium**: 150x60px (3 studs)
- **Large**: 200x60px (3 studs)
- **Square**: 100x100px (4 studs in 2x2)
- **Tall**: 60x150px (3 studs vertically)

### Visual Effects

1. **Hover Effect**: Bricks slightly enlarge and lift when hovered
2. **Drag Effect**: Bricks become semi-transparent while dragging
3. **Snap Animation**: Gentle bounce when snapping into place
4. **Sparkles**: Multi-colored sparkles appear when placing bricks

### Background

- **Color**: Calming sky blue gradient
- **Purpose**: Creates positive, open feeling
- **No Distractions**: Plain background keeps focus on bricks

## Audio Design

### Sound Effects (Placeholders Included)

1. **Pickup Sound**
   - **When**: Clicking on a brick
   - **Character**: Soft, gentle "pop"
   - **Duration**: ~0.3 seconds

2. **Place Sound**
   - **When**: Releasing a brick
   - **Character**: Soft "thud"
   - **Duration**: ~0.5 seconds

3. **Snap Sound**
   - **When**: Brick snaps to another brick
   - **Character**: Satisfying "click"
   - **Duration**: ~0.4 seconds

### Audio Principles

- **Volume**: Low (30% max) to avoid startling
- **Tone**: Soft and positive
- **Optional**: Can be muted if needed
- **No Background Music**: Keeps environment calm

## Educational Benefits

### Motor Skills Development

1. **Hand-Eye Coordination**: Aligning cursor with target
2. **Fine Motor Control**: Precise mouse movements
3. **Grip Strength**: Mouse clicking and holding
4. **Spatial Precision**: Placing bricks in specific locations

### Cognitive Development

1. **Spatial Awareness**: Understanding how objects fit together
2. **Problem Solving**: Figuring out how to create structures
3. **Planning**: Thinking ahead about next brick placement
4. **Creativity**: Free-form building encourages imagination

### Emotional Development

1. **Confidence**: No failure means building confidence
2. **Independence**: Can play without adult help
3. **Pride**: Creating something tangible
4. **Patience**: Slow, deliberate actions

## Building Suggestions

While the game has no enforced goals, children might naturally create:

### Tower
- Stack bricks vertically
- Mix different colors
- See how high they can build

### House
- Start with large bricks as foundation
- Add walls with medium bricks
- Top with square bricks as "roof"

### Train
- Line up bricks horizontally
- Use different colors for each "car"
- Add tall bricks as "smoke stack"

### Abstract Art
- Random placement
- Color patterns
- Overlapping designs

## Safety Features

### Data Privacy
- No data collection
- No analytics
- No network requests
- Completely offline

### Content Safety
- No text (prevents inappropriate content)
- No user-generated content
- No social features
- No in-app purchases

### Physical Safety
- Encourages breaks (no addictive loops)
- No flashing lights
- No loud sounds
- Calm, slow pace

## Technical Specifications

### Performance

- **Target FPS**: 60

- **CPU**: Minimal usage
- **Startup Time**: <3 seconds

### Compatibility

- **Platform**: Windows 7+
- **Screen**: 1024x768 minimum
- **Input**: Mouse only
- **Audio**: Optional
